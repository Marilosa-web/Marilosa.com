On board

A voyage with Marilosa

A passage on Sweden’s High Coast, from Härnösand to Trysunda, with a captain and one crew member. What Marilosa says at each step, and what stays with the crew.

An illustration of the concept. Times and values are invented, and Marilosa is not yet a working product.

At the dock09:30 · before departure
Prevent
MarilosaBefore we leave: who is on board today? A first name, age and sex for each person help rescuers find and call them in poor visibility. It stays on board unless there is an emergency.

Each of them runs through IMSAFE privately on their own phone. Both declare “fit for the voyage”. Both answer yes to “Comfortable with this plan?”

MarilosaWind rises to 11 m/s on the open stretch after 15:00. Leaving at 10:00 gets you in by about 13:30. Fuel, lifejackets and charts: checked. Your call.

The captain decides to leave at 10:00.

Underway11:40
Detect
MarilosaWind has been 3 m/s above the forecast for an hour.
CrewEngine room, rattle.
MarilosaRecorded. Starboard engine temperature is normal but rising slowly: 82 to 86 °C in 20 minutes. I’ll watch it closely.
A concern12:15
Respond
MarilosaI’m concerned. Three things are adding up: the starboard engine is at 91 °C and rising, the wind is above the forecast, and the last sheltered harbour before the open stretch is 4 nautical miles away. Options: continue on the port engine at 6 knots, arriving 14:10; turn into the shelter, 35 minutes; or slow down and check the raw-water strainer once in calm water. Your call.

The captain turns into the shelter. In calm water the crew finds a plastic bag in the raw-water strainer and clears it. The temperature falls back to normal. Had they needed help, Marilosa had a situation brief ready in SBAR order, for the crew to read out when they called.

After arrival15:20 · Trysunda
Learn
MarilosaA short SAIL debrief? Situation: you planned to arrive by 13:30 and arrived at 15:10, after a stop in the shelter. Actions: the rattle was reported early, and the strainer was cleared. What should we change?
CrewCheck the strainer before every passage.
MarilosaProposal: add “raw-water strainer” to this boat’s pre-departure check. Approve?

The captain approves. The lesson goes into the log, and the next voyage starts better prepared.
