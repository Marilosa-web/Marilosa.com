About

Why we build Marilosa

Marilosa helps small crews enjoy safe, well-prepared voyages, and care for the sea they sail on.

Why Marilosa?

The name echoes mariposa, Spanish for butterfly. Every word in it was chosen for a reason.

Marine

The word of boats, their equipment and the sea itself. We want crews to care for the sea they sail on.

Learning

The hull of the model. Every voyage and every season makes the next one better.

Operations

The crew’s real work: preparing, sailing and maintaining the boat.

Safety

The outcome we are after, for the people aboard and the sea around them.

Advisor

Not an assistant that acts, but an advisor that informs. Marilosa advises. The captain decides.

Where we are

Marilosa is a concept in development, not yet a working product. Version 1 is for privately used motor yachts with a small crew, and for a captain sailing alone.

We welcome conversations with research partners, captains and crews interested in taking part in trials.
