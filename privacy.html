Visiting the website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like most web hosts, GitHub records the IP address of visitors in its logs for security purposes. We do not receive or use these logs. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you email us

We use your email address and what you write only to reply and to continue the conversation you started. Email to marilosa.com is received by one.com and forwarded to a Gmail account (Google). We do not share your message with anyone else and delete it when it is no longer needed for the conversation.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. Write to the address above. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
